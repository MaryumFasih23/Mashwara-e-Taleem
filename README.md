
# Mashwara-e-Taleem
### Your Personalized Study Abroad Advisor

Mashwara-e-Taleem is an AI-powered study abroad advisory platform designed to simplify the process of exploring international education opportunities.

Choosing the right university, academic program, and scholarship can be overwhelming. Mashwara-e-Taleem brings these resources together in one platform, helping students make informed decisions based on their academic backgrounds, interests, and preferences.

Developed as a **Final Year Project (FYP)** for the BS Computer Science program at **FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES), Islamabad**.

##  Dataset Overview

Mashwara-e-Taleem incorporates extensive educational datasets to support university exploration, program discovery, and scholarship recommendations.

| Dataset | Records |
|---------|--------:|
| US Universities | ~700 |
| Non-US Universities | ~300 |
| Academic Programs | 36,149 |
| Scholarships | 1,975 |

These datasets form the foundation of the platform's educational discovery and recommendation capabilities.

##  Problem Statement

Students planning to pursue higher education abroad often encounter several challenges:

- **Fragmented information:** University, program, and scholarship details are scattered across numerous websites.
- **Limited personalization:** Generic university rankings rarely account for individual academic profiles.
- **Time-consuming research:** Evaluating institutions and eligibility requirements requires significant manual effort.
- **Scholarship discovery barriers:** Finding relevant financial aid opportunities can be particularly difficult.

Mashwara-e-Taleem aims to address these challenges through a unified, personalized study abroad advisory experience.

---

##  How It Works

1. **Student Profile**  
   Students provide their academic background, interests, and study preferences.

2. **Educational Exploration**  
   The platform uses its university, program, and scholarship datasets to identify relevant opportunities.

3. **Personalized Recommendations**  
   Students receive suggestions aligned with their profiles and educational goals.

4. **Informed Decision-Making**  
   Students explore their options and identify potential pathways toward international higher education.
